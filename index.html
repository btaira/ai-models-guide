Read the labels correctly

“Open” is not one thing.

Open weights

You can download parameters. That alone says nothing about the training documents, mixture, preprocessing, or reproducibility.

Categories disclosed

The developer names broad sources such as web, code, licensed, or synthetic data, but does not provide the document-level corpus.

Full corpus

Data or reconstruction artifacts, mixtures, processing code, and training details are available for meaningful audit and replication.

When source files are undisclosed

Black-box membership and “data archaeology” studies can produce evidence that a model likely encountered a book, dataset, or document. They are probabilistic, model-version-specific, and vulnerable to confounding. They do not recover a full pretraining corpus.
